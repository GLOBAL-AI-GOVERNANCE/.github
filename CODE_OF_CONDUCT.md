# Code of Conduct

Participate professionally, respectfully, and in good faith.

Expected behavior includes constructive technical disagreement, evidence-aware claims, respect for privacy and intellectual property, and attention to the safety and dignity of people affected by the work.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate deception, doxxing, credential exposure, or attempts to use project channels to facilitate abuse are not acceptable.

Repository maintainers may moderate or remove contributions that violate these expectations.
