# Protected Concern Boundary

Public repositories record governance state and bounded product evidence. They are not intake channels for confidential reports, source identities, personal data, private evidence, or sensitive allegation details.

A credible material concern may change assurance validity. When a protected concern exists, the public state may record only the minimum product-safe disposition needed to prevent stale assurance from being treated as current.

Public artifacts must not contain reporter identity, protected-source identity, raw protected reports, private evidence, or confidential investigative detail. Sensitive handling belongs outside public Git surfaces.

The public consequence is bounded:

- prior assurance may become `QUESTIONED` or `STALE`;
- revalidation may become required;
- consequential progression may be held or stopped;
- public records must not imply that the concern was resolved unless bounded evidence supports that result.

Automated checks detect declared public-surface violations. They do not adjudicate reports, establish truth, certify safety, or replace accountable human authority.
